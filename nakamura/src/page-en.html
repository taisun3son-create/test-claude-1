Nakamura Seitai

A small bodywork clinic in Toyonaka, Osaka, 3 minutes on foot from Okamachi Station (Hankyu Takarazuka Line). We treat neck, shoulder and lower back pain with gentle hands-on work.

What to expect

On your first visit (60 minutes), we look at your posture and movement, explain what is causing the pain, and suggest how often to come. Then we treat. No cracking, no machines.

The director, Kei Nakamura, worked in an orthopedic clinic for ten years. He can explain things in simple English. If you prefer, you can also ask for our female practitioner, Sayaka Mori.

Prices (tax included)

  • First visit, 60 min: 5,500 yen
  • Regular treatment, 40 min: 4,400 yen
  • Postnatal pelvic care, 40 min: 4,950 yen

Cash, credit cards and PayPay are accepted. Japanese health insurance cannot be used.

Hours

  • Saturday: 9:00–17:00 (last entry 16:00)
