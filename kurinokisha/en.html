Kurinoki-sha

A twelve-seat café in a 90-year-old miso storehouse in Obuse, Nagano. House-roasted coffee and sweets made with Obuse chestnuts.

About us

We roast our beans every morning at the back of the storehouse, and bake with chestnuts from farmers in town. Take your time — there is no rush here.
