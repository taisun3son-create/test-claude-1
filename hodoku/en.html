Tsujido, Fujisawa

Gentle bodywork, one knot at a time.

Hodoku Bodywork is a small, appointment-only clinic six minutes on foot from Tsujido Station. One therapist, one client, no rush.

About

What we do

We help with stiff shoulders and neck, lower back pain, tension headaches, and postnatal hip and back care. Pressure is slow and gentle; we never crack joints or push through pain.

Your therapist, Saki Miyashita, is a licensed judo therapist (a Japanese national qualification) with nine years in hospital rehabilitation. She speaks simple English, and a translation app is always on hand.

Prices (tax included)

First visitConsultation and body check, then 60 minutes of treatment. About 90 minutes in total.7,700 yen
Bodywork 60 minOur standard session from the second visit.6,600 yen
Bodywork 30 minNeck and shoulders only, or lower back only.3,850 yen
Postnatal care 60 minFrom two months after birth. Your baby is welcome in the room.6,600 yen

Bodywork in Japan is not covered by national health insurance. Cash, credit cards and QR payments are accepted.

Getting here

6 minutes on foot from the south exit of JR Tsujido Station (Tokaido Line), toward the sea. Two parking spaces in front of the clinic. We will send you the exact address when you book.

How to book

Use the online form (you can write in English), or call us at 0466-00-0000. We will reply within 24 hours, except on Wednesdays.
